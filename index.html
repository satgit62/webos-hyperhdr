HyperHDR for LG webOS

Control your HyperHDR Ambilight setup directly from your TV – no additional devices required.

Features

  • Direct connection to HyperHDR
  • Real-time Ambilight control
  • Adjust brightness, colors and effects
  • Optimized for LG webOS

Requirements

  • Running HyperHDR instance
  • LG webOS TV with Homebrew Channel
  • Root access may be required

HyperHDR for webOS

by satgit62

Bring your Ambilight experience to the next level. Control your HyperHDR setup directly from your LG webOS TV – no additional devices required.

✨ Features

  • LUT Loader - The menu provides access to pre-made LUT templates for SDR, HDR, and Dolby Vision
  • Direct connection to your HyperHDR server
  • Real-time Ambilight control
  • Adjust brightness, colors and effects
  • Fast and lightweight
  • Optimized for TV usage

⚙️ Requirements

  • Running HyperHDR instance in your network
  • LG webOS TV with Homebrew Channel
  • Root access may be required
